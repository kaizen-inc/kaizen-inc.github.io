Information collected automatically

  • Usage & technical data — such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and interactions with the Services.

We use the information we collect to:

Our applications may allow you to sign in or connect through third-party identity providers and platforms. When you authorize such a connection, we receive only the information you consent to share, as governed by that provider's own privacy policy and permission scopes. We use this information solely to provide the functionality you requested and do not sell it.

We may rely on trusted third-party service providers (for hosting, analytics, and infrastructure) who process data on our behalf under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:
